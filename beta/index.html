Add Linger to Chrome

It takes about 2 minutes. You need the Chrome browser on a laptop or desktop computer.

  1. Download Linger. Download Linger for Chrome
  2. Open the file you downloaded. It's called linger-extension.zip, in your Downloads folder. On a Mac, double-click it. On Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All. You now have a folder called linger-extension.
  3. Open Chrome's extensions page. Copy this, paste it in Chrome's address bar, and press Enter: chrome://extensions
  4. Turn on Developer mode. It's a switch in the top-right corner of that page.
  5. Click Load unpacked. Choose the linger-extension folder from step 2, then click Select.
  6. Pin Linger so it's easy to find. Click the puzzle piece 🧩 at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Linger.

Done! Open any website: hard words get a dotted underline. Click one, or highlight any sentence, to see what it means.
