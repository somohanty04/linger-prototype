Privacy Policy

For the Linger phone app (website and iPhone app) and the Linger Chrome extension. Last updated November 19, 2026.

In short: Linger keeps your saved words and settings so it can help you learn. You don’t need an account. Photos are read on your phone. Linger has no ads, sells nothing about you, and doesn’t track what you do. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app.

What stays on your device

Your saved words, settings, reading setup (what you read, your languages and English level) and learning progress are kept on your phone or computer. If you don’t make an account, they never leave your device, except as described below.

If you make an account (optional)

An account keeps your words the same on every device. We use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store:

  • your email address, and your password, which Firebase keeps in a protected form that we can’t read
  • your saved words: the word, its meaning, the example sentence, where you saw it (for example a website’s name or “Camera scan”) and the date
  • your reading setup and the result of the optional English check
  • your learning progress for each word
  • a profile picture, only if you add one

Other people using Linger can’t see it: the database only lets your account read your data. The people who run Linger can see it in the Firebase console, and only look to fix problems. If you connect the Chrome extension to your phone with a pairing code instead of an account, your saved words are stored under that code, and anyone who has the code can see them.

Photos, scans and screenshots

When you scan with the camera, upload a photo, or share a screenshot to Linger, the text is read on your device. The picture isn’t uploaded or kept. Only text you then ask about (a word or phrase and its sentence, or the text you translate) is sent, as described under AI below. The camera is only on while you’re scanning, and Linger doesn’t record video.

AI explanations and translations

Some features use AI. In the phone app: explaining a word or phrase you tap or highlight (with the sentence it’s in), translating meanings or the text you scanned into your language, and writing a guide for a city you search for. In the Chrome extension: explanations and the page assistant, which you turn on and agree to first. For these, only the text involved (a word or phrase and its sentence, the scanned text you chose to translate, a city name, or the page you asked about) is sent to Linger’s server, which passes it to Anthropic’s Claude API to write the answer. Linger’s server doesn’t store this text. To stop overuse, it briefly remembers your internet address and a random ID made by the app, in memory only. Anthropic handles API data under its own privacy policy.

Word lookups

When you tap a word Linger doesn’t already know, the word (only the word) is sent to Wiktionary to find its meaning.

Say it (pronunciation)

When you tap the microphone in Say it, your voice is turned into text by speech recognition: Apple’s in the iPhone app, or your browser’s on the website (for example, Chrome sends audio to Google). Linger only gets the text of what was heard, uses it to score your try, and doesn’t keep it. The microphone is only on while you’re saying a word.

Your settings

Display and accessibility settings (text size, zoom, dark mode, high contrast, color filter, reduced motion) are kept on your device only. City guides that Linger AI writes for you are also kept on your device.

Your location

If you tap “Use my location” in the city guide, your phone’s location is used on your device to pick the nearest city. It isn’t sent anywhere or saved.

Eye tracking and hand gestures (Chrome extension)

These optional features use your webcam. The video is processed on your computer and never leaves it.

Beta feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, we store your message, the type you picked, your email (only if you type it), the app version, your phone type, the screen you were on, a random tester ID and basic browser details. We use it only to improve Linger.

Services that load parts of the app

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, the Firebase code from Google, and the text reader from jsDelivr. Like any website, these services see your internet address when they send those files.

What we don’t do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No analytics that follow what you do in the app.
  • We don’t sell or share your data with anyone, except the services named above that make the features work.

Delete your data

  • Account: tap your profile picture, then Delete account and data. This deletes your profile, reading setup, learning progress, picture and saved words, and then the account itself.
  • On your device: deleting the app, or clearing the website’s data in your browser, removes what’s stored there.
  • Feedback or pairing codes: send a message from Settings → Send feedback asking us to delete it, with the email you used if any.

Children

Linger is made for adults and isn’t meant for children under 13.

Changes and questions

Linger is in beta, and this policy may change as features change. We’ll update the date at the top when it does. For questions, use Settings → Send feedback in the app.
