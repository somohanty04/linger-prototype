Linger

Your pause is the search bar.

For adults who speak English as a second language and moved to the US. Linger spots hard words, local slang and confusing letters, explains them in context and for your area, reads them aloud, and helps you practice the words you meet.

Try it: the app opens on Lens. Tap the round button to turn on the camera, fit a letter, sign or bill in the frame, and tap again to scan it. No camera? Use Upload a photo or Paste text. Tap any underlined word for its meaning, then press Start Activity on Words to practice.
