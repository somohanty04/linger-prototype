Linger

Your pause is the search bar.

For adults who speak English as a second language and moved to the US. Linger spots hard words, local slang and confusing letters, explains them in context and for your area, reads them aloud, and helps you practice the words you meet.

Try it: on Lens, open the lease letter and tap Explain this letter or Read aloud. Tap an underlined word for its meaning. Choose 📷 Camera to scan a real letter, or Prefer texting? for the WhatsApp helper. Then press Start Activity on Words to play Fill in the story.
