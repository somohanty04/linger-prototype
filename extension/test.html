City of Pittsburgh · Notice to Tenants

Dear Resident, the Department of Permits, Licenses, and Inspections will commence annual inspections of rental premises on November 12, 2026. Landlords are obligated to ensure that each unit is accessible during the scheduled window.

Tenants with delinquent utility balances must remit payment no later than December 1. Failure to pay may result in termination of service. A penalty of $45 will be assessed on overdue accounts, pursuant to the lease agreement.

Subletting is prohibited without prior written consent from the lessor. Please retain this notice for your records. Questions? Call 311.
