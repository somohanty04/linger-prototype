Fix eye tracking

  • Light in front of you. A lamp or window in front of you, not behind you. A dark room makes your eyes hard to see.
  • Face the screen. Sit about an arm’s length away, like you did when you set it up.
  • Glasses glare. If your glasses reflect the screen or a lamp, tilt them slightly or move the light.
  • Only one face in view. Linger follows one person. Someone next to you can confuse it.
  • Camera not in use. Close Zoom, Teams, FaceTime or other apps that might be using the camera.

Still off after checking these? Recalibrate: follow the dot for about 35 seconds.

Hand gestures

  • 👆 Point with your index finger to move the pointer. Hold it on a word and Linger asks if you want help.
  • ✋ Swipe an open hand right for yes, left for no.
  • ✋ Swipe an open hand up or down to scroll.
  • 🤏 Pinch your thumb and index finger, hold, then move up to zoom in or down to zoom out.
