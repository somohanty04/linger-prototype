Try each one

  1. ✓👆 Point with your index finger. A dot follows it.
  2. ✓Hold the dot on a word until its ring fills. Linger asks “Want help with this word?”
  3. ✓✋ Swipe an open hand to your right for yes, or your left for no.
  4. ✓✋ Swipe an open hand up to scroll down, or down to scroll up.
  5. ✓🤏 Pinch your thumb and index fingertip together, hold a moment, then move up to zoom in or down to zoom out. Open your fingers to stop.

These settings apply on every site, same as in the Linger menu. Hand gestures only work in the tab you’re looking at.
